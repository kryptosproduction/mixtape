FAQ

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes. The free plan includes one playlist, the full player, the mini player, and listening stats. Pro unlocks unlimited playlists and extra features.

Can it play songs that aren't on Spotify?

Yes — that's the point. Anything on YouTube (live covers, indie, regional music, remixes) can go into a Mixtape playlist.

Why does it open a YouTube tab?

Mixtape plays through your own YouTube tab in the background so playback is reliable and within YouTube's terms. You never have to look at it — you control everything from the sidebar.

Is my data private?

Your playlists and stats stay on your device and sync via your own Chrome account. Nothing is sold or sent to us. See our Privacy Policy.

What browsers does it support?

Google Chrome (and Chromium-based browsers that support extensions and the side panel).
